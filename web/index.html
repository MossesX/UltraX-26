UltraX 26

Pro video recorder & video calls for the Galaxy S26 Ultra

Video calls in the browser

If someone sent you a call link from the UltraX 26 app, just open it — you join from this page, no app or account needed. You can also start a call from here and share the link with anyone (including UltraX 26 phones).

The app

  • Every Camera2 option the phone exposes — all lenses, up to 8K, 10-bit HDR, high frame rates, full manual control, Samsung vendor keys.
  • Hands-free start / pause / stop by gestures (thumbs up/down, claps, blinks, nods…), sounds (claps, snaps, whistles) and voice commands.
  • AR effects: filters, costumes, accessories, virtual and 3D backgrounds, beauty and age looks — in recordings and in calls.
  • Video calls with anyone, Meet-style: share a link, they join in a browser. Calls are peer-to-peer and encrypted.

Source code and APK builds: github.com/MossesX/UltraX-26
